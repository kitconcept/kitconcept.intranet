# Watch content

This guide shows you how to watch a page so you get an email whenever it
changes, how to stop watching it, and how to list everything you watch.

## Prerequisites

- You are logged in, and you have an email address in your profile.
- Your administrator has turned on watching content
  (see {doc}`enable-content-watching`).

## Watch a page

1. Open the page you want to follow.
2. Find the watch button:
   - On a **page, news item, or event**, it's in the top right of the
     **About this content** box, near the bottom of the page.
   - On a **file or image**, it's the **eye** next to Like, below the content.
3. Select **Watch**.

   A confirmation appears, and the button turns dark and says **Watching**.

```{image} /_static/images/watch-content-toast.png
:alt: Confirmation that you are now watching the content
```

```{image} /_static/images/watch-content-watching.png
:alt: The "About this content" box with the pressed "Watching" button
```

From now on, you get an email each time someone else changes the published
page. The email tells you who changed it, when, and why (if they entered a
change note), and links to the page and to its version history so you can see
what changed.

## Stop watching a page

1. Open the page.
2. Select **Watching**.

   A confirmation appears, and the button goes back to **Watch**. You get no
   more emails about this page.

:::{tip}
Each watch email ends with a reminder of how to stop watching: open the page
and select the eye.
:::

## List the content you watch

Create a page that lists everything you watch, for example "My watched content":

1. Create a page and add a **Listing** block.
2. In the block settings, add the criterion **Watched by** and choose
   **Watched by me**.
3. Save the page.

The listing shows only the items **you** watch—each user who opens the page
sees their own list. Select an item to open it, and stop watching it there.

## Troubleshooting

- **I don't see the watch button.** You aren't logged in, the feature is turned
  off, or the content type can't be watched.
- **I don't get emails.** Check that your profile has an email address and that
  the page is published. You don't get emails about your own changes.
