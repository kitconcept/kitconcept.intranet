# Read and manage your notifications

This guide shows you how to read your notifications, mark them as read, and see what changed.

## Prerequisites

- You're logged in. Anonymous visitors have no notifications.

## See how many notifications are unread

Look at the bell in the tools bar at the top right of the page, or in the compact header of a workspace.
The red badge shows the number of unread notifications.
Above nine, it shows **9+**.
With no unread notifications, there's no badge.

## Read your latest notifications

1.  Click the bell.
    The dropdown lists your five latest notifications.
2.  Click a page title to open the page.
    The notification is marked as read.

## Read all notifications

Open the overview page in one of these ways:

- Click the bell, then **See all notifications**.
- Open your personal menu in the toolbar and click **Notifications**.
- Go to `/notifications`.

The page lists all your notifications, newest first, grouped into **Today**, **Yesterday**, and older dates.
At the bottom, **Load more** shows older notifications.

To list only unread notifications, switch to the **Unread** tab.

## Mark notifications as read

- **One notification:** click the check mark on its right, or open its page.
- **All notifications:** click **Mark all as read**, in the dropdown or on the overview page.

You can't mark a notification as unread again.

## See what changed

For a notification about an edit, click **View changes**.
The page's version history opens, where you can compare the versions.

## Troubleshooting

**A notification disappeared.**
You can no longer view its page: the page was deleted, or it's now private.
Notifications about such pages are hidden and don't count as unread.

**A notification doesn't show up right away.**
The bell refreshes when you open another page, and every minute.
Open another page, or wait a minute.

**Several edits show as one notification.**
Edits by the same person on the same page within 15 minutes are merged into one notification.
