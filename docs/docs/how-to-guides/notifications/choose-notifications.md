# Choose which notifications you get

This guide shows you how to switch a type of notification on or off.

## Prerequisites

- You're logged in.

## Steps

1.  Go to `/notifications`, for example through **See all notifications** in the bell's dropdown.
2.  Click the gear button next to **Mark all as read**.
    **Notification preferences** opens.
3.  Select the types you want, and clear the ones you don't:

    | Preference | You're notified when |
    |------------|----------------------|
    | Someone likes content I author | a colleague likes one of your pages |
    | Someone edits content I author | a colleague edits one of your pages |
    | Someone changes the state of content I author (publish, retract, …) | a colleague publishes, retracts, or otherwise changes the workflow state of one of your pages |

4.  Click outside the popover, or press {kbd}`Esc`, to close it.

```{image} /_static/images/notification-center/preferences.png
:alt: The notification preferences popover with one checkbox per notification type
:width: 600px
```

Each change is saved right away.
There's no save button.

## Result

From now on, you only get the types you selected.
The preferences apply to new notifications only: notifications you already have stay.
Your preferences are personal and don't change what colleagues get.
