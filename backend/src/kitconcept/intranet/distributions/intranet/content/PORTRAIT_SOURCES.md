models are not the named people and do not endorse this project.

[Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/) on 2026-09-10 and reframed for
the profile image field. Ben Yilmaz's portrait uses AI-assisted outpainting of
the original photograph to provide enough space for a circular crop.
